DTCE Tool Kit Access Admin Privacy Policy

Effective: 5 November 2026 · Last updated: 6 November 2026

This policy covers the native iPhone app DTCE Tool Kit Access Admin and these support pages. The app is a companion utility for authorized administrators of the Windows DTCE Tool Kit.

The app processes signing keys and access codes on your device. It does not transmit your imported key, export password, request codes or generated approvals to a developer-operated server.

Information you provide

You may select an encrypted mobile signing-key export through the iOS Files picker, enter its export password, paste a Windows request code, choose program permissions and enter a Windows version for certification access. The app uses this information locally to import your key and generate signed access approvals.

The export password is used during import. The app does not intentionally save it in app settings or send it to the developer. Request text and selections may remain in the current app session. Generated output is cleared when the app locks.

Signing-key storage and Face ID

After a successful import, the signing key is stored in an iPhone Keychain item protected by the device passcode and the current Face ID enrollment. The Keychain item is configured for this device only. The app locks when moved to the background and after five minutes without interaction.

Face ID authentication is performed by iOS. The app and developer do not receive your face images or biometric templates. Changing Face ID enrollment can make the saved key inaccessible and require importing your original encrypted export again.

The original export remains wherever you stored it in Files. Your chosen file provider, such as iCloud Drive, may store or synchronize that file according to its own settings and privacy policy.

Optional demo mode

Demo mode uses a newly generated, temporary signing key held in memory. It does not use or replace your imported administrator key, and its key is not saved in the Keychain. Demo output is marked DEMO and cannot activate the Windows User app. Exiting demo mode or locking the app discards the demo session and its output and restores the previous input choices.

Collection, tracking and advertising

The app does not include advertising, cross-app tracking or an additional analytics SDK. It does not operate an online user-account service. The developer does not receive a copy of your signing key or in-app request and approval history through the app, and does not sell that information.

Apple may process App Store or TestFlight download information, usage information and crash diagnostics under Apple's services, privacy terms and applicable device settings. The developer may receive reports Apple makes available. These Apple-provided reports are separate from the app's on-device signing operations.

Copying, sharing and other services

When you choose Copy or Share, the selected approval code is placed on the clipboard or passed to the app or recipient you select. Those services and recipients control their own handling and retention. An approval code can grant its holder the access stated in the code until its expiry; share it only with the intended recipient.

The app uses iOS Files, Keychain, Face ID and system sharing features. Any storage or communication service you choose is governed by that provider's terms. The app does not automatically upload your signing key to a developer service.

Retention and deletion

Use Remove saved key in the app to delete its stored signing key from the iPhone Keychain. Keep a safe backup of your original encrypted export if you need to import it again. Uninstalling an iOS app may leave Keychain items behind, so use the in-app removal control before uninstalling if you want to remove the saved key.

Removing the saved key does not remove original exports in Files, clipboard contents or copies already shared with other people or services. Previously generated approvals remain subject to their original validity and expiry. Manage external copies through their respective apps or providers.

Support correspondence

If you email support, you voluntarily provide your email address, message and any attachments. That information is used to respond to your request and investigate the issue. Email is also processed by the email providers involved. Do not include a signing key, key export, export password or a usable activation code in a support message.

This website

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process request information, such as an IP address and browser details, to serve and protect the website under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. These pages do not contain additional analytics or advertising scripts. Visiting the website does not upload your app's signing key.

Changes and contact

This policy will be updated when relevant data practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision.

For privacy questions or requests concerning information you voluntarily provided to support, contact developer@dtce.online.
