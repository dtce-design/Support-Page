InfraSnap Support

Email developer@dtce.online for help with installation, access, photo capture, saved photos or exports.

When requesting help

Include your device model, iOS version, and InfraSnap version/build from Settings → About. Explain what happened. Only attach photos you are authorized to share, and remove private project information where possible.

Capture requirements

Allow Camera and precise location while using the app. Capture requires an internet time check and the GPS accuracy threshold chosen in Settings. Try an open area if the GPS fix is not accurate enough.

Saving and backup

Allow the required Photos access to save and update your InfraSnap photos. Keep your own backups. NAS backup is optional and requires a trusted HTTPS WebDAV endpoint that you configure.

Access and privacy

No account, login or access password is required. The app is being prepared for unlisted distribution in the Philippines; anyone who receives the download link may be able to use it.
