InfraSnap Privacy Policy

This policy describes the iPhone and iPad app InfraSnap. Contact: developer@dtce.online.

Photos and project information

InfraSnap stores your captured photos, project details, notes and preferences on your device. It saves photo copies to Apple Photos when you grant the required permission. The developer does not operate an InfraSnap photo-upload or analytics service in this release.

Precise location

With permission, InfraSnap uses precise location while the app is open to add GPS coordinates and related information to your field photos. Coordinates and capture times can be visible in the watermark and custom barcode and may also be written into image metadata. Anyone receiving the photo may be able to read this information. Messaging apps can remove metadata, but visible stamps and barcodes may remain.

Network services

The app checks internet time using an HTTPS request to Apple. Apple's location and reverse-geocoding services may process your location to supply positioning or address information. Opening an online map sends requests to Esri or OpenStreetMap tile services; requests can reveal your IP address and the map area being viewed. Those services process requests under their own privacy policies. An offline Cagayan map is also included.

Sharing and optional backup

Photos and exports leave the app when you choose to share them or configure a backup. Optional NAS backup uploads your selected original photos to the HTTPS WebDAV server you configure. The selected service or server operator controls its own retention. NAS credentials are stored in the device's secure storage. They are used to connect to your chosen server.

Permissions and access

Camera is used for photo capture. Photos access supports saving, viewing, editing and sharing. Location is used while the app is open; the app does not request background or Always location permission. Motion information supports camera orientation. Local-network access may be used for a configured NAS. You can manage permissions in iOS Settings.

Tracking, advertisements and accounts

This release contains no advertising or advertising-tracking SDK and does not request cross-app tracking permission. It does not create an online user account. The shared startup/weekly access password is checked locally. This is not a claim that third-party network services receive no request data.

Control and deletion

You can edit or remove photos and project records in the app and manage permissions in iOS Settings. Deleting a local item does not automatically remove copies already shared, exported or backed up. Uninstalling the app removes its local app data; photos saved in Apple Photos and external backups are managed separately. Contact support about app behavior or questions concerning this policy.

Changes

This policy will be updated when relevant data practices change. Version: App Store candidate 3.9.10.
